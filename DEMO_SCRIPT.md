# AI SafeHome Three-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

“Falls at home can be dangerous for older adults, but it is hard to know what to change in a room. AI SafeHome turns one room photo into a simple safety plan for older adults, families, and caregivers.”

## 0:20–1:15 — One safety check

Choose a room and upload a staged room photo. Point out the photo-quality feedback, AI-marked hazard locations, and one hazard card. Explain that the app asks follow-up questions only when the photo cannot clearly answer something.

## 1:15–1:55 — Score and safety plan

Show the risk score, color bar, and risk label. Then show the three-step safety plan. Explain that the score is capped at 100, considers hazards one at a time, and keeps category values as a backup when an AI severity score is unavailable.

## 1:55–2:25 — Accessibility and safety

Open Accessibility. Show large text, high contrast, and an optional Read Aloud button. Open Privacy and AI information. Say that photos are not saved to the app database, the app is not medical advice, and a person should seek qualified help for serious concerns.

## 2:25–3:00 — Progress over time

Show a saved Room Name with its trend chart and a before/after comparison. End with: “AI SafeHome does not replace human judgment. It makes the first safety check easier to understand and act on.”
