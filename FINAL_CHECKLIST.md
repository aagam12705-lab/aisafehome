# AI SafeHome Pre-Submission Checklist

## App quality

- [ ] A judge can finish a demonstration in under three minutes.
- [ ] A staged photo gives consistent, understandable results.
- [ ] The risk score, color bar, and three-step safety plan are easy to find.
- [ ] Every button and page has plain-English wording.
- [ ] There is no raw HTML, developer error, or deprecated Streamlit warning visible.

## Accessibility

- [ ] Light, Dark, System, and High Contrast modes have readable text and controls.
- [ ] Large and Extra Large text do not cut off important controls.
- [ ] Read Aloud works for results, follow-up questions, safety plan, and report.
- [ ] Phone layout has no sideways scrolling.

## Privacy and safety

- [ ] Use staged, non-patient photos for the demo.
- [ ] Privacy and AI limitations are visible and accurate.
- [ ] Uploaded photos are not saved to the app database or attached to email.
- [ ] Passwords and service/API keys are not committed to GitHub.
- [ ] The README and DATA_POLICY match the deployed app behavior.

## Submission readiness

- [ ] README includes purpose, feature list, architecture, run steps, and privacy details.
- [ ] TESTING.md checks the full flow, mobile layout, accessibility, and saved checks.
- [ ] Demo video opens with the problem and ends with the safety plan.
- [ ] Student can explain AI uncertainty, the 100-point score cap, the fallback score values, and the role of human review.
- [ ] Deployed link and source-code link are available for judges.
